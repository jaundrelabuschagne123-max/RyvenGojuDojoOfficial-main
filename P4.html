Goju-Ryu Karate Kumite (sparring)

What is Kumite?

Kumite, meaning "sparring," is the practical application of Goju-Ryu Karate techniques against an opponent. It develops timing, distance, speed, control, awareness, and confidence while teaching students to apply their skills safely and effectively. Kumite helps karateka improve both their self-defence ability and overall understanding of karate.
