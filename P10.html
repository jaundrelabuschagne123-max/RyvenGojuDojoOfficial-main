Ranks & Grading

The Goju-Ryu ranking system from Kyu grades through the Dan ranking levels.

Understanding the Ranking System

Progression in Goju-Ryu is traditionally represented through Kyu and Dan grades. Kyu grades represent the coloured-belt stages of student development, while Dan grades represent progression through the black-belt levels.

Each grade represents another stage of technical development, knowledge, discipline, experience, and responsibility.

Kyu Ranking — Student Grades

The Kyu system progresses from 10th Kyu toward 1st Kyu, followed by the transition into the Dan ranking system.

The Journey Through Rank

Rank in Goju-Ryu represents more than the colour of a belt. Each stage of progression reflects continued training, technical development, discipline, knowledge, and personal growth.

The progression from Kyu to Dan is a continuing journey. Achieving a higher rank represents increased responsibility as well as increased technical and personal development.
