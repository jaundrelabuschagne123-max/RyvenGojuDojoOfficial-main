Kihon

Goju-Ryu Karate Kihon (Basics)

What is Kihon?

Kihon (基本), meaning "fundamentals", is the foundation of Goju-Ryu Karate. It includes the practice of basic stances, strikes, blocks, kicks, and movement. Through regular training, students develop proper technique, balance, power, coordination, and discipline. A strong kihon forms the basis for all kata, kumite, and self-defence, making it essential for every karateka, from beginner to black belt.

Basic Stances

Sanchin Dachi, Zenkutsu Dachi, Shiko Dachi, Han Zenkutsu Dachi, Neko Ashi Dachi.

Basic Punches

Oi Zuki, Tate Zuki, Ura Zuki, Fura Zuki, Gyaku Zuki

Basic Blocks

Age Uke, Soto Uke, Uchi Uke, Gedan Baraai, Sukui Uke, Kake Uke, Hike Uke, Teisho Uke.
