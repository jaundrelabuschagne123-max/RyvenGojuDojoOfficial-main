Goju-Ryu Karate Katas.

What is Kata?

Kata (形), meaning "form", is a sequence of traditional movements that teaches the principles and techniques of Goju-Ryu Karate. Kata develops balance, timing, breathing, power, focus, and discipline while preserving the art's techniques. Regular practice helps karateka improve both physical skill and mental concentration, making kata an essential part of Goju-Ryu training.

The traditional Goju-Ryu kata are (click for youtube videos):

Gekisai Dai Ichi Gekisai Dai Ni Saifa Seiyunchin Shisochin Sanseru Seisan Sepai Kururunfa Suparinpei Sanchin Tensho

Today, Goju-Ryu Karate is practised throughout the world by people of all ages. Whether training for self-defence, competition, fitness, or personal growth, practitioners continue to follow the traditional principles established by Chojun Miyagi. Goju-Ryu remains a martial art that develops not only physical ability but also discipline, respect, resilience, and lifelong self-improvement.
